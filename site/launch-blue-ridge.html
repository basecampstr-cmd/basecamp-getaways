About the Cabin Keeper partnership

Two different sets of eyes on your cabin.

What Basecamp already does

Our team is in your cabin at every turnover, before and after each clean, and walks it periodically through the season. That’s a guest-readiness inspection: damage, wear, cleanliness, supplies, anything affecting the next stay. It’s included in management at no extra cost and it doesn’t change.

What Cabin Keeper adds

Cabin Keeper is an independent Blue Ridge–area partner providing a substantially higher level of inspection between our touchpoints — propane levels and leaks, power outages and resets, HVAC, furnace and water heater, plumbing and freeze risk, window and door seals, and storm damage assessment after heavy weather, with a written report each visit.

What it normally costs: Cabin Keeper sets its own pricing, ranging from roughly $90 per visit pay-as-you-go up to about $460 per month for their most comprehensive plan, depending on service level and property. The Qualification Standard

$85,000 projected — not $85,000 already earned.

The Launch Package is available for properties Basecamp reasonably projects, at the time of enrollment, can generate at least $85,000 in annual Gross Booking Revenue — assuming you complete the required improvements and provide the required availability.

That’s deliberately a projection standard, not a trailing-performance standard. A brand-new or newly renovated property with no rental history can qualify on day one.

Gross Booking Revenue is not your take-home

GBR is a gross measure used only for qualification and the Performance Guarantee. What you actually receive — your Owner Distribution — is revenue due to you less Basecamp’s management fee, the technology fee and reimbursable operating expenses such as cleaning, supplies, maintenance and vendor charges, all itemized on your monthly owner statement.

To qualify, a property must:

  • Be accepted as a new Basecamp Getaways management property
  • Be projected at enrollment to generate $85,000+ in annual Gross Booking Revenue
  • Enter into the required Basecamp management agreement
  • Participate in Basecamp’s 25% management program
  • Pay the applicable $120 technology fee
  • Allow Basecamp to control pricing, minimum stays, promotions and revenue strategy
  • Meet Basecamp’s minimum rental-availability requirements
  • Complete agreed-upon recommended property improvements
  • Maintain the property, furnishings and amenities to guest-ready standards
  • Maintain all legally required permits, licenses and insurance
  • Remain current and in good standing with Basecamp
  • Use Basecamp as the property’s exclusive short-term-rental manager

Qualification and package availability are determined by Basecamp based on projected revenue, condition, location, amenities, owner-use requirements and overall rental potential.

The Three Commitments Behind the Offer

Performance requires availability.

These three commitments are what make the Launch Package economics work. Each is a condition of eligibility, and each is documented in your management agreement.

Availability

Designated Peak Season dates stay open for guests, and owner use stays within the agreed annual allowance.

Condition

Required improvements are completed on schedule and the home stays in guest-ready condition.

Control

Basecamp sets pricing, minimum stays and promotions, and is your exclusive short-term-rental manager.

Peak Season is published as a dated exhibit

Peak Season isn’t left to interpretation. Basecamp publishes the exact dates annually for each market as a numbered exhibit to your management agreement — for example, Exhibit B — 2027 Peak Revenue Dates (Blue Ridge) — and you acknowledge those dates by signature. A replacement exhibit is issued each calendar year. Outside Peak Season, owner use is permitted up to the maximum owner-blocked nights set in your agreement.

The Details That Matter

How the numbers actually work.

Two things decide whether these packages are worth anything to you: how the Performance Guarantee is measured, and how the Property Upgrade reimbursement vests. Both are defined below, and both are set out in full in the terms document.

A Fair Test Needs a Full Season

How the Performance Period is measured.

A lake house that goes live in October has never had the chance to sell a week in July. So the Performance Guarantee isn’t measured over any twelve months — it’s measured over twelve months that contain every designated Peak Season date, each with a real booking window in front of it.

  • Launch Readiness Date — the later of go-live across all channels and verified completion of required improvements.
  • Ramp Period — a minimum of 90 days. Ramp stays settle normally under your agreement and don’t count toward the target.
  • Performance Period — the 12 full calendar months that follow, which must include every Peak Season date with 90+ days of prior open availability.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can I combine two packages?

No — one Launch Package per qualifying property. Packages cannot be combined, transferred to another property, or exchanged for cash, unless Basecamp agrees in writing.

Doesn’t Basecamp already inspect my cabin? Why Cabin Keeper too?

We do, and we’ll keep doing it. Our team is in your cabin at every turnover — before and after each clean — and we walk it periodically through the season. That catches what a guest-facing eye catches: damage, wear, supplies, anything that would affect the next stay.

Cabin Keeper is a different kind of look. They’re a Blue Ridge–area partner doing a deeper, systems-level check between our touchpoints — the things that quietly go wrong in an empty mountain cabin. Propane levels and leaks. Power outages and resets. HVAC, furnace and water heater. Plumbing and freeze risk in a cold snap. Window and door seals. Storm damage after heavy weather. It’s the check you’d want done in February when nobody has been up in three weeks.

What does Cabin Keeper normally cost, and what exactly is covered?

Cabin Keeper prices their own service, and it ranges from roughly $90 per visit on a pay-as-you-go basis up to about $460 per month for their most comprehensive plan, depending on the level of service and the property. Under this package, Basecamp covers that cost for your first qualifying 12-month period, up to the $5,000 combined package value.

Which plan fits your cabin, and exactly which Cabin Keeper services are included, is agreed with you at Launch Package selection and documented in your signed management agreement — so there’s no ambiguity about what’s covered before you commit. Add-on work Cabin Keeper bills separately, such as contractor coordination, materials or concierge requests, is not included.

My property is brand new with no rental history. Can it qualify?

Yes. The $85,000 standard is based on Basecamp’s projection at enrollment — assuming you complete the required improvements and provide the required availability — not on revenue the property has already earned.

What happens if I sell the property or leave early?

You keep everything already earned and simply stop earning more. That’s true whether you terminate, Basecamp terminates without cause, or you sell. If the buyer assumes the management agreement with our approval, vesting continues under the new owner.

The one exception is termination for Cause — things like fraud, booking around us, cancelling confirmed guests, or blocking Peak Season dates without approval. In that case, promotional value already provided becomes repayable, capped at $5,000.

Do I have to give up my own time at the property?

No, but Peak Season dates must stay open for guests unless we approve owner use in advance and in writing. Outside Peak Season, you get an agreed allowance of owner-blocked nights — commonly around 30 per rolling 12-month period, with a sub-limit on weekends. The exact allowance is set for your property.

Is the Performance Guarantee a $5,000 check?

No. It’s up to $5,000 in credits applied against future Basecamp management fees, equal to the revenue shortfall. It can never result in a cash payment to the owner.

What does the management program cost?

The Launch Package requires Basecamp’s qualifying 25% management program plus the applicable $120 technology fee. Those are separate from, and not reduced by, the promotional Launch Package.
