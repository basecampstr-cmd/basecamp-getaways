Questions owners ask

The honest answers.

If yours isn’t here, call us — the number is at the bottom of this page.

Is the projection really free?

Yes, free and no obligation. You get the projection, a listing analysis, and a custom quote whether or not we ever work together. We only earn when your home books.

What do you charge?

A percentage of what your home actually earns — no setup fee, no monthly retainer, nothing before your first booking. We’ll give you the exact number with your projection, because the right percentage depends on your home and your market.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No multi-year lock-up. We’d rather keep your home by earning it every month. We talk terms openly and put them in writing before anything’s signed.

Can I still use my own house?

Yes. It’s your home. Block the weeks you want on the calendar and we work around them. We’ll be honest with you about which weeks are expensive to hold so you can decide with real numbers in front of you.

I already have a manager. Is switching a headache?

It’s the most common move we make, and we handle it. We take over the listings, keep existing reservations intact so no guest gets disrupted, re-shoot the home, reset pricing, and put local staff on it. Star Mountain came to us from a manager charging 35% and did $96K the following year.

I self-manage on Airbnb. Why hand it over?

Usually for two reasons: the 11pm calls, and the ceiling. We run pricing daily, market directly to every guest who has ever stayed with us, and list your home on our own market booking sites, where guests book direct free of guest fees. Most owners who switch aren’t just buying their evenings back — they end up above what they were doing alone.

Who handles cleaning, maintenance and the 2am problem?

We do. Vetted local crews on a turnover schedule built around guests, our own people for maintenance, and a real person answering the phone when something goes wrong at an unreasonable hour. You find out afterwards, in a message that says it’s already handled.

Which markets do you cover?

Lake Hartwell, Lake Keowee, Lake Murray, Blue Ridge GA, Edisto and the Greenville area. If your home is near one of those, we’re local to it.

What happens after I submit the form?

Within two business days you get a real projection for your property and your market, along with your listing analysis. Then an honest conversation about whether Basecamp is a good fit — including when we think it isn’t. If we are, you get a custom quote for your home. No cost, no obligation, no pressure at any point.
