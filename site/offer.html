Qualification

Does your property qualify?

We measure on what your property can earn, not what it has earned — so a home with no rental history at all can qualify on day one.

Properties Basecamp reasonably projects, at the time of enrollment, can generate at least $85,000 in annual Gross Booking Revenue — assuming you complete the required improvements and provide the required availability.

What happens next

Four steps, and nothing signed until you’ve seen real numbers.

1

We build your projection

We’re analyzing your property, its market and its comparable set, and preparing your complimentary Basecamp Vacation Rental Income Projection. It typically comes back to you within two business days.

2

We schedule your call

We’ll reach out to book a time and walk the projection through with you line by line — including whether the property clears the $85,000 projected standard and qualifies for the Launch Package.

3

You choose your package

If it qualifies, you pick one of the four Launch Packages. On the same call we’ll agree any required improvements with a written scope and deadline, your Peak Season dates, and your owner-use allowance.

4

We sign and launch

Your selected package, agreed revenue target and Exhibit B peak dates go into the management agreement. Your Launch Package begins when the property goes live.

Important Promotional Disclosure. The Basecamp Launch Package provides up to $5,000 in promotional value and is available only to qualifying new management properties accepted by Basecamp Getaways. Properties must generally be projected by Basecamp, at the time of enrollment, to generate at least $85,000 in annual qualifying Gross Booking Revenue — assuming completion of required property improvements and provision of required rental availability — and must satisfy property-condition, improvement, rental-availability, owner-use and management-program requirements.

Participation requires Basecamp’s qualifying 25% management program and the applicable $120 technology fee. Owner-blocked dates are restricted, including restrictions during designated Peak Season dates as published for each market and acknowledged by the owner as an exhibit to the management agreement. Certain Basecamp-recommended property improvements may be required as a condition of eligibility. Under Package 01, hot tub service is up to four scheduled services per qualifying 12-month period, labor only, capped at $1,500, and requires a working hot tub on site; dock-moving benefits are available only to qualifying lake properties with an existing dock and are capped at $2,500. Amounts unused for one service remain available for the others, subject to each sub-limit.

Benefits vary by selected package, have no cash value except earned reimbursements expressly provided under the Property Upgrade Package, and are subject to package-specific limits and terms. If Basecamp terminates for Cause as defined in the management agreement, or the owner terminates while a Cause condition remains uncured, all promotional value already provided becomes immediately due and repayable to Basecamp, capped at $5,000 per property. In all other termination scenarios the owner retains benefits already received and forfeits only future unvested benefits.

Revenue projections are estimates and are not guarantees except as expressly provided under the separate Performance Guarantee terms. Credits under the Performance Guarantee are applied against future Basecamp management fees, capped at $5,000 in the aggregate, cannot result in a cash payment, and may be voided by the owner’s failure to satisfy the guarantee conditions. Gross Booking Revenue is a gross measure and does not represent amounts distributable to the owner. Additional terms and conditions apply. This page is a summary for informational purposes; the Basecamp management agreement and its exhibits contain the binding terms and control in the event of any inconsistency.
