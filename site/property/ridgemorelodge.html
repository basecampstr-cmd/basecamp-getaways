About this home

⭐ Highlights
🏡 Premium lakefront home on Lake Hartwell — sleeps 17 · 7 BR · 5 full baths · 3 levels.

🚤 Covered deep-water dock (5+ ft depth) — easy swimming, boating & paddling. Solar dock lights, paddleboards, kayaks, lake floats, plus a fishing rod & tackle for the anglers.

🔥 Outdoor living — multiple covered porches over the lake, a lower-level firepit for s'mores and stargazing, a grill for cookouts, and an outdoor TV for game day.

🎮 Basement rec room — couch & big-screen TV, ping pong, a karaoke machine, and a full bar area with wine-cooler fridge & ice maker.

👨‍🍳 High-end chef's kitchen — premium appliances, fully stocked cookware, blender and Keurig coffee, ready to cook for a crowd.

Tile-shower en-suites and elevated finishes throughout, with multiple living areas so everyone can spread out.

📍 Up the street from Currahee Club and minutes from the Stephens County recreation area. It's an easy drive to historic downtown Toccoa for dining, shops and the Ritz Theater. Curahhee is a private golf club. You're close to Tugaloo State Park, Toccoa Falls, and Currahee Vineyards & Winery. Ingles grocery is in Toccoa, and the closest boat ramp is just 1–2 miles away.

🛏️ Sleeping (sleeps 17):
• Main-level primary suite — king, lake views, en-suite bath, deck access
• Main-level queen bedroom — hall bath nearby
• Three upstairs queen bedrooms, each overlooking the lake (one en-suite + one hall bath)
• Upstairs bunk/family room — 4 twin XL beds
• Basement bedroom — 2 twin daybeds, full bath on this level
5 full baths total.

🎯 Great for large family vacations, multi-generational trips, reunions, golf getaways (up the road from Currahee Club), fishing trips and lake weekends.

Pet-friendly for dogs (fee applies) — please keep pets off the furniture or use a provided cover. No smoking indoors. Lake levels naturally fluctuate, so please use care swimming and around the dock ladder.
